# FuzzyRoutines 2.1.0 production acceptance

**The 2.1.0 production release is published and verified.** The maintainer
merged promotion PR #348 and documentation PR #354, then pushed annotated tag
`v2.1.0` (tag object `662f0e8b3faad1fbbc3c284cc04677c66d57aa96`) on approved
source `bbc47529cf1e0baa17d74b0f008d26f5b36ef68b`. The final documentation tree
matched the independently reviewed PR and its ten successful opening workflows.

The [protected production workflow](https://github.com/Fuzzy-Technologies/FuzzyRoutines/actions/runs/39241460028)
passed all seven jobs: reproducible wheel/sdist builds, full tests and clean
distribution installs on CPython 3.13 and 3.14, provenance attestation, OIDC
publication and both post-publication verification jobs. Each full source
suite reported **1,671 passed and 22 explicit skips**. The configured `pypi`
environment was approved after the annotated tag, exact source and downloaded
CI artifact hashes were checked.

[PyPI 2.1.0](https://pypi.org/project/fuzzyroutines/2.1.0/) received the wheel at
11:05:37 UTC and the source distribution at 11:05:39 UTC on 19 October 2026.
Both post-publication jobs downloaded both files, verified their actual sizes
and SHA-256 hashes against the CI candidates, then installed the hash-pinned
wheel from PyPI into a fresh environment and ran the documented executable
checks. The two retained [3.13](evidence/release-2.1-production/published-artifacts-3.13.json)
and [3.14](evidence/release-2.1-production/published-artifacts-3.14.json) byte-verification
reports agree exactly.

The [GitHub Release](https://github.com/Fuzzy-Technologies/FuzzyRoutines/releases/tag/v2.1.0)
was published at 11:07:22 UTC with the existing Alice illustration and the exact
CI wheel, sdist, `SHA256SUMS`, `reproducibility.json` and `source-revision.txt`.
Its distribution asset digests match both PyPI reports. The two post-publication
verification ZIP archives retain the downloaded distributions and installed-example
outputs beyond the temporary Actions artifact retention period.

The [publication record](evidence/release-2.1-production/publication.json) binds
the tag, source, production jobs, attestation, release assets, artifact digests
and raw report hashes. Earlier candidate measurements and scientific acceptance
remain historical evidence for their named revisions. This release does not
establish private FMA or robot migration acceptance. Subsequent documentation
maintenance and branch synchronization do not move the release tag or replace
the immutable PyPI distributions.
